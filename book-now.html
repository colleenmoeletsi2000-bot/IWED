How Online Booking Works

  1. Select Your Service

    Choose from pickup & delivery, ironing & folding, or dry cleaning — or combine them.

  2. Pick a Date & Time

    Select your preferred collection date and time slot. We offer morning (7–11 AM) and afternoon (12–5 PM) slots.

  3. Enter Your Details

    Provide your address, contact number, and any special instructions for your laundry.

  4. Pay Securely Online

    Pay via card, EFT, or SnapScan. Your booking is confirmed once payment is received.

  5. We Collect & Deliver

    Sit back and relax. We'll send you a confirmation and keep you updated via SMS or WhatsApp.

Pickup & Delivery Booking

Our pickup and delivery service covers Johannesburg, Sandton, Midrand, East Rand, and Pretoria. Book at least 3 hours in advance to guarantee your slot.

What to prepare:

  • Place your laundry in a bag or basket.
  • Separate delicates or dry-clean items in a separate bag and label them.
  • Include any special instructions (stain treatment, temperature preferences, etc.).
  • Leave the bag at your door or security gate at the agreed time.

Available time slots:

  • Morning slot: 7:00 AM – 11:00 AM
  • Afternoon slot: 12:00 PM – 5:00 PM

Delivery fee: Free on orders over R200. R50 for smaller orders.

Dry Cleaning Booking

For dry cleaning bookings, please fill in the form below or contact us directly. Our team will confirm your order, provide a quote for any specialist items, and arrange collection.

Important dry cleaning notes:

  • Always check the care label — if it says "dry clean only", do not wash at home.
  • Inform us of any stains and when they occurred — fresh stains are easier to remove.
  • Wedding dresses and leather items require a special consultation before booking.
  • Standard turnaround is 48 hours. Express 24-hour service costs 40% more.
