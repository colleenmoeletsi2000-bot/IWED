Our Policies

Cancellation Policy

You may cancel or reschedule a pickup booking up to 2 hours before the scheduled collection time without any penalty. Cancellations made within 2 hours of collection may incur a R30 administration fee. No-shows without prior cancellation will be charged the R30 fee.

Lost or Missing Items Policy

FreshFold takes great care with every item. In the unlikely event that an item is lost or missing, you must report it to us within 48 hours of receiving your delivery. We will investigate and, if the item was lost due to our error, compensate you for up to the reasonable replacement value of the item. We strongly recommend checking your order upon return.

We are not responsible for items left in pockets (keys, cash, earphones, etc.). Please check all pockets before handing over laundry.

Damage Policy

While we handle all garments with care, some fabrics may be affected by standard washing processes. We always check care labels and sort appropriately. If damage occurs due to our handling, notify us within 24 hours of delivery with photos. Compensation will be assessed based on the item's condition and estimated value. We are not liable for damage to garments with deteriorated fabric, undisclosed stains, or items sent to the wrong service type by the customer.

Quality Guarantee

If you are unhappy with the quality of your wash for any reason, contact us within 24 hours of delivery and we will re-wash the affected items at no charge. This guarantee applies once per order. We are not able to re-wash items that have been worn or re-soiled after delivery.

Privacy Policy

FreshFold Laundry collects personal information (name, address, phone number, email) solely for the purpose of processing and delivering your orders. We do not share, sell, or distribute your personal information to third parties. Your payment information is processed through a secure third-party payment gateway and is never stored on our systems. By booking with us, you consent to receiving order confirmations and service updates via SMS or WhatsApp. You may opt out at any time.

Payment Terms

Online bookings must be paid in full before the order is confirmed. For drop-off orders, payment is accepted in cash or card at the facility. Business account clients are invoiced monthly with a 14-day payment term. Late payments on business accounts may attract interest.
