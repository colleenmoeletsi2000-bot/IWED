Got questions? We've got answers. Browse the sections below or contact us if you need more help.

Pricing Questions

How is the price calculated?

For wash and fold orders, we charge by weight (per kilogram). We weigh your laundry when we collect it and send you a final invoice with the exact weight and cost before processing payment. Ironing is charged per item, and dry cleaning per garment type.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. We are completely transparent about our pricing. The only additional charges you might encounter are:

  • Delivery fee of R50 on orders under R200 (free above R200).
  • Express surcharge of 40% for same-day turnaround on dry cleaning.
  • Specialist item fees for items that require special handling (e.g., wedding dresses, leather).

You will always be informed of these costs before your order is confirmed.

Do you offer discounts for first-time customers?

Yes! New customers receive 10% off their first order when they use the promo code FRESH10 at checkout. The discount applies to wash & fold and ironing orders only (not dry cleaning).

Can I get a quote before booking?

Absolutely. Contact us on 011 000 1234 or WhatsApp 082 000 1234 and describe your laundry needs. We'll give you an accurate estimate before you commit.

Is there a minimum order?

Yes, the minimum order for wash and fold is 3 kg. There is no minimum for ironing-only or dry cleaning services.

Do you charge extra for heavily soiled clothes?

In most cases, no. Our standard wash process handles everyday dirt, sweat, and light stains at the standard rate. For heavily soiled items, grease stains, or stains requiring specialist pre-treatment, we may charge a small additional fee — but we will always let you know first.

Can I cancel or change my package at any time?

Yes. You can cancel or change your package with at least 7 days' notice before your next billing date. There are no cancellation fees. Simply contact us via email or phone to make changes to your subscription.

Do unused washes in a package roll over?

No. Unused washes and credits within your monthly package do not roll over to the following month. We encourage customers to use all included loads each month to get the best value.

Do you have any seasonal or referral offers?

Yes! We run seasonal promotions around the start of school terms, the festive season, and Easter. We also have a Refer a Friend programme: refer a friend who books their first order and you both receive R50 credit on your next order. Follow us on social media or sign up to our mailing list to stay updated on offers.

Can businesses get a corporate account?

Yes. Our Business Package includes monthly invoicing, a dedicated account manager, and flexible billing. We also work with Airbnb hosts, guest houses, salons, and medical practices. Contact us to set up a corporate account.

Service Questions

How long does laundry take?

Standard wash and fold orders are returned within 24 hours of collection. Dry cleaning takes 48 hours standard. Express 24-hour dry cleaning is available on request at an additional cost.

What happens if an item gets damaged?

We take every precaution to care for your garments. However, in the rare event of damage caused by our handling, we will compensate you up to the value of the item (with proof of purchase). For high-value items, we recommend informing us so we can take extra care. All claims must be reported within 24 hours of delivery.

Do you wash all types of fabric?

We wash most common fabrics including cotton, polyester, linen, denim, and blended fabrics. Delicate materials like silk, cashmere, and wool are handled using our specialist dry cleaning service. Always check your garment's care label before sending it to us.

What if I'm not happy with my laundry?

We offer a quality guarantee. If you're not satisfied with the result, contact us within 24 hours of delivery and we'll re-wash your items free of charge. No questions asked.

Do you separate whites and colours?

Yes, always. Our team sorts all laundry by colour and fabric type before washing. If you want to be extra careful, you're welcome to pre-sort your laundry into separate bags before collection.
